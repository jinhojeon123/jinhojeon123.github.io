# Fatou's Lemma

## Statement

If $\{  f_n \}$ iis any sequence in $L^{+}$, then 
$$
    \int \liminf f_n  \le {\liminf \int f_n }
$$ 
## Proof

<!-- how to build f1 <= f2 <= f3 <= ...  -->

Let $ 
g_n(x)=\inf_{k\ge n} f_k(x).
\ $   


for all $ n \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}, \\ $

$$
    0 \le g_n \le g_{n+1}  
$$

By MCT, 
$$
    \int { \lim g_n} = {\lim} \int g_n
$$



On the left side, 

$$
    \int {\liminf f_n }
$$

On the right side,

$$
    \lim \int g_n
$$

However, 

$$
    g_n \le f_k \quad \forall k \ge n
$$

By Monotonicity, 

$$
    \int g_n \le \int f_k 
$$

and by inf 

$$
    \int g_n \le \inf_{k\ge n} \int f_k  \quad (\because \ g_n \le \inf_{k\ge n} f_k)
$$


and by lim 

$$
    \lim_{n\to \infty} \int g_n \le \lim_{n \to \infty} \inf_{k\ge n} \int f_k 
$$

Then by definition of $\liminf$, 

$$
        \lim_{n \to \infty} \inf_{k \ge n} a_k = \liminf_{n \to \infty} a_n
$$


Hence, 

$$
    \lim_{n\to \infty} \int g_n \le \lim_{n \to \infty} \inf_{k\ge n} \int f_k = \liminf_{n\to \infty} \int f_n 
$$

Since, 

$$ 
    \int \liminf_{n \to \infty} f_n = \lim_{n \to \infty} \int g_n 
$$

Therefore, 

$$
\int \liminf_{n\to\infty} f_n
\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\int f_n.
\qquad\square
$$

# Dominated Convergence Theorem

## Statement

Let $\{ f_n \}$ be a sequence in $L^{1}$ such that 
$$
    \begin{aligned}
        (a)\:& f_n \to f \quad \text{a.e.},\\
        (b)\:& \exists g \in L^{1} \quad s.t \ |f_n| \le g \ \text{a.e.} \ \forall n. 
    \end{aligned}
$$

Then $f\in L^{1}$ and $\int f = \lim_{n \to \infty}  \int f_n$
