# Dominated Convergence Theorem

## Statement

Let $\{ f_n \}$ be a sequence in $L^{1}$ such that 
$$
    \begin{aligned}
        (a)\:& f_n \to f \quad \text{a.e.},\\
        (b)\:& \exists g \in L^{1} \quad s.t \ |f_n| \le g \ \text{a.e.} \ \forall n. 
    \end{aligned}
$$

Then $f\in L^{1}$ and $\int f = \lim_{n \to \infty}  \int f_n$
