I am **Jin Ho Jeon**, preparing for graduate study in computational and mathematical sciences. This site records my study of mathematics and numerical computation, with an emphasis on connecting derivations to implementations.

## Current focus

My published notes currently cover measure theory and convergence, floating-point arithmetic, optimization, steepest descent, and conjugate gradients. My next study directions are numerical PDEs and FEM, preconditioning, domain decomposition, and parallel sparse computation.

The organizing path is **mathematical foundations → numerical methods → solvers → parallel implementation → research experiments**.
